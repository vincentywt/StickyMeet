01 — How it works

One link, shared in the chat you already use.

Step 1

Create a link

Set the time, place and a commitment between HK$50 and HK$200. Share it on WhatsApp or in Carousell chat.

Step 2

They commit

Paid from the wallet they already use. A licensed payment provider holds the funds. StickyMeet never does.

Step 3

Check in

A GPS geofence and a 6-digit code at the spot. Location is read only when you tap, never tracked.

Step 4

Settled automatically

Both there: a full refund within minutes. A flake: the commitment goes to whoever showed up.

05 — FAQ
Do you hold my money?

No. A licensed payment provider collects and refunds every commitment. StickyMeet only tells it whether to refund or pay out, based on check-in.

What if GPS doesn't work?

Upload a photo from the spot instead. A real person reviews disputes within 24–48 hours, and payouts wait until it's resolved.

What is MeetupScore?

A 0–1000 reputation built from completed meetups and flake rate. It's earned, never bought.

When can I use it?

We're soft-launching in Hong Kong with waitlist members first, starting with tradespeople, tutors and power sellers.
