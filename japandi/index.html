How it works

A gentle nudge with real weight. No full-price holds, no new app to install, just one link you share in the chat you're already using.

Questions

Do you hold my money?

No. A licensed payment provider collects and refunds every commitment. StickyMeet only tells it whether to refund or pay out, based on check-in.

What if GPS doesn't work?

Upload a photo from the spot instead. Disputes go to a real person, who reviews them within 24–48 hours, and payouts wait until it's resolved.

What is MeetupScore?

A 0–1000 reputation built from completed meetups and flake rate. It's earned, never bought.

When can I use it?
