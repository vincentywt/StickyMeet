Enquiries

Questions, answered.

Do you hold my money?

Never. A licensed payment provider collects and returns every commitment. StickyMeet merely issues the instruction.

Should GPS falter?

A photograph from the venue suffices. A person reviews each dispute within 24–48 hours.

What is MeetupScore?

A reputation from 0 to 1000, earned through completed meetups. It cannot be purchased.

When may I begin?

A private soft launch in Hong Kong, by invitation to the waitlist.
