Do you hold my money?

No. A licensed payment provider collects and refunds. StickyMeet only sends the refund or payout instruction.

What if GPS fails?

Upload a photo from the spot. A real person reviews disputes within 24–48 hours.

What's MeetupScore?

A 0–1000 reputation from completed meetups and flake rate. It can't be bought.
